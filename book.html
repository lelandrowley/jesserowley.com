Printing it

At home

  • Letter paper, turned sideways (landscape).
  • Print at actual size, 100 percent, not “fit to page.”
  • For both sides of the sheet, choose “flip on short edge.”
  • The dark pages use a lot of ink, and a home printer leaves a white border around them.

At a print shop

  • Give them the PDF as it is and ask for letter size, landscape, colour, both sides.
  • Heavier paper helps: the dark pages show through thin sheets.
  • It binds along the short left edge.

Fair warning

  • This edition has been checked on screen only. Nobody has printed and bound a copy yet, so try a few pages before printing all 153.
  • The photographs are family prints and Army pictures of the time. Some are small and will look soft on paper.

What stays on the website

  • The maps that play the fighting day by day, and the 3D terrain. The book has a still map in their place.
  • The recording. On the main page every quotation opens the video at that moment; on paper each one gives the time instead.
  • Corrections. The site is changed as the family adds to it, and the book is rebuilt from it. The date above says how current this edition is.
